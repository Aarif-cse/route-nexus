eRTMAC-NWIS: Nearby Wells Intelligence System

⚠️ The Subsurface Problem Statement (SIH26121)

During drilling operations, superintendents spend over 72 to 100 hours per well manually reviewing unstructured Daily Drilling Reports (DDRs), mud logs, and paper folders. Critical geological hazards—such as differential stuck pipe and abnormal pressure kicks—are frequently discovered only after striking the drill bit, generating catastrophic Non-Productive Time (NPT) costing $45,000–$120,000/day.

💡 The Route Nexus eRTMAC Solution

An autonomous multi-page intelligence platform that integrates 100+ offset wells, applies NLP extraction on legacy DDRs, models geomechanical hazard curves 300m in advance, and streams real-time drilling parameter recommendations directly to rig consoles.
